This question asks for one specific thing. Give that thing precisely and
directly, with its citation. If the thing is a person, place or object ("who
is", "what is"), say what the sources state about it: what it is, its role,
and its key facts. Do not open with background the sources do not require, do
not list what else the sources mention, and do not round the answer out with a
summary paragraph. If the sources state the value alongside a detail
that qualifies it, give the value and the qualifying detail — not every
adjacent fact you can find.
