This question asks about more than one thing side by side.

- Answer each side from sources about that side. Do not fill one side's column
  with facts about the other, and do not reach for what you know about one
  because the sources were thin on it.
- Then state the contrast explicitly: where they agree, where they differ, and
  by how much if the sources give a figure for both.
- Name the side you are on for each claim, so a reader can tell the two apart
  without reading twice.
