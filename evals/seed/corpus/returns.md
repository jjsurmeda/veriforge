# Aurora Widget Returns Policy

## Standard returns

Unwanted AW-2000 units may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a
full refund, provided they are unused and in the original packaging.

## Engraved units

Engraved or customised units are only returnable within 14 days of
delivery and carry a 15% restocking fee.

## Faulty units

Faulty units are replaced free of charge within the warranty period; a
fault report must describe the fault code shown by the status LED.

## Shipping

Return shipping is paid by Aurora for faulty units and by the customer
for unwanted units.
