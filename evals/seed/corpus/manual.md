# Aurora Widget AW-2000 User Manual

## Package contents

The AW-2000 ships with the widget unit, a USB-C charging cable, a quick-start
card, and a mounting bracket. The unit weighs 250 g with the bracket attached.

## Setup

Charge the unit for two hours via USB-C before first use. The status LED
glows amber while charging and green when fully charged.

## Battery

The AW-2000 provides up to 10 hours of continuous operation on a full
charge. Battery health degrades to 80% of original capacity after roughly
500 full charge cycles.

## Operating conditions

Safe operating temperature is -10 °C to 50 °C. The enclosure is rated
IP54: protected against dust and splashing water. Do not submerge.

## Pairing

Up to 5 devices can be paired with one widget. To pair a new device, hold
the pair button for 3 seconds until the LED blinks blue.

## Reset

Hold the reset button for 10 seconds to factory-reset the widget. All
paired devices are forgotten.
