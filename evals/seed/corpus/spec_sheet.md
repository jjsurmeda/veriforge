# AW-2000 Product Family Specification Sheet

## Models

| SKU | Description | Battery | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AW-2000-X | Standard | 10 h | 220 g |
| AW-2000-XP | Pro | 20 h | 260 g |
| AW-2000-XE | Enterprise | 20 h + external pack | 260 g |

## Environmental ratings

The AW-2000-X is rated IP54 with an operating range of -10 °C to 50 °C.
The AW-2000-XP is rated IP67 with an operating range of -20 °C to 60 °C
and may be briefly submerged to 1 m.

## Radio and security

All models operate on 2.4 GHz with AES-256 encryption. Firmware version
3.2.1 or later is required for the enterprise management protocol.

## Charging

Full charge takes 1.5 hours on all models when using a 30 W USB-C
adapter.

## Fault codes

E-41 indicates blade motor overcurrent. E-42 indicates battery
temperature out of range. E-43 indicates a failed over-the-air firmware
update.
