# Aurora Widget Warranty Policy (2025 edition)

## Coverage

Aurora Widgets Pty Ltd warrants the AW-2000 product family against
manufacturing defects for 24 months from the date of purchase.

## Claiming

A valid claim requires proof of purchase and the serial number printed
under the battery door. Claims are lodged through the support portal.

## Exclusions

The warranty does not cover damage from submersion (the enclosure is
splash-resistant only), unauthorized disassembly, or normal wear of the
mounting bracket. Consumables such as the cutting blade (part RP-77) are
covered for 90 days only.
