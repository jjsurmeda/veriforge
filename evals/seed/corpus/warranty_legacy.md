# Aurora Widget Warranty (legacy terms, pre-2025)

This document reflects warranty terms for units sold before 1 March 2025.

## Coverage period

Legacy units are covered for 12 months from the date of purchase.

## Transfer

The legacy warranty transfers once to a second owner if the original
invoice is presented. The 2025 policy does not permit transfer.

## Servicing

Legacy claims must be lodged by post; the 2025 portal is not available
for legacy units.
