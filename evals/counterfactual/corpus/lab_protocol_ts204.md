Tessellate Materials Ltd, Cork. Protocol TS-204, revision 4.2.
Applies to all ceramic precursor preparation from received powder.

## Procedure

1. Weigh the dry powders to the amounts in the reagent table. A batch is
   **217 g** of dry powder plus binder and dispersant.
2. Wet-mill for **48 hours** at **320 rpm** with a 2:1 ball-to-powder mass
   ratio, in deionised water to a solids loading of 61 %.
3. Dry the slurry in an oven at **210 °C** for **36 hours**, to a residual
   moisture below 0,2 %.
4. Disintegrate with a mortar and pestle, then pass through a **45 µm** sieve.
5. Add binder and dispersant as a 3 % solution at 18 g of solution per 100 g of
   powder, and mix for 25 minutes.
6. Measure viscosity at **25 °C** and **500 rpm**. The specification band is
   **1 800 to 2 600 mPa·s**. A batch outside the band is rejected.
7. Press at **220 MPa** into 30 mm discs, holding pressure for 3 seconds.
8. Sinter at **1 640 °C** with a **4 hour** dwell, then cool at
   **180 °C per hour** to below 900 °C before removing the load.
9. Record the fired density. The acceptance band is **5,900 to 6,050 kg/m³**.

## Safety

The mill must be bolted down before use and the jar interlock verified with a
firing blank before every batch. The sintering furnace may not be opened above
**760 °C**. Pressurised binders are supplied in 1 L containers at 4 bar and must
be vented behind the sash.

## Deviations

A deviation from any value in the equipment or reagent table requires a
written deviation note before the batch starts, countersigned by the process
lead. Deviations to the drying oven temperature and to the sintering dwell may
be approved by the process lead; deviations to the press pressure and to the
sieve mesh may not be approved at batch level and require a protocol change.
