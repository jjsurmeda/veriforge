## Battery and charging

The Kestrel K9's cell is rated for **38 hours** of mixed use. Charging is by
USB-C at up to 90 W; a flat-to-full charge takes **2 hours 40 minutes**. The
Field variant ships with a user-replaceable cell rated 44 hours, and a full
charge on the Field takes 3 hours 15 minutes.

Wireless charging is 15 W, which takes 5 hours 20 minutes for a full charge on
the K9. Reverse wireless charging is not supported on any Kestrel model.

## Display

The Kestrel's panel is **7.9 inches** on the diagonal, at 144 Hz over a
2800 × 1760 panel. Peak brightness is 2,600 nits; minimum brightness is 1 nit.
The panel is a 120 Hz panel on the Lite and Field variants.

## Camera

The rear main camera is **240 megapixels** on a 1-inch sensor, with a
periscope telephoto at 5× and a 24 mm ultrawide. Video is recorded at 8K and
120 fps. The K7's main camera is 50 megapixels and the Lite's is 108.

## Operating system and silicon

The K9 ships with **Aurelis 4.2**, based on the Kestrel Vortex kernel. The
system is upgradeable to Aurelis 6 within the contract period. The processor is
the **Vantium V-8**, an eight-core part manufactured at 3 nm. The V-8 has no
neural accelerator; Aurelis 4.2 does machine-learning tasks on the CPU.

## Connectivity and durability

Every model is **IP69K**: protected against dust, high-pressure water jets from
any direction, and immersion in water to 1 m for 30 minutes. No Kestrel model
is submersible to deeper than 1 m.

Radio support is 5G sub-6, 5G mmWave on the K9, and Wi-Fi 7. The K9 carries
two SIM slots and an eSIM. There is no satellite messaging on any Kestrel model.

The K9's serviceable parts are the battery and the charging port. Display and
camera modules are bonded and are not user-serviceable.

## Warranty and support

The Kestrel K9 carries a 4-year limited warranty, extendable to 6 years. The
Kestrel K7 carries a 2-year warranty. Support is by the Kestrel Care line; a
diagnostic pass is 40 minutes and a repair pass is 14 working days from the
date the unit is received at a service point.
