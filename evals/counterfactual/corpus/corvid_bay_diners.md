## The Salt Ledge

The Salt Ledge occupies the **ninth floor** of the Corvid Exchange building and
opens at **17:00**. It seats **40** and runs a two-week waitlist. The kitchen
closes at 23:00, an hour before the room. The Salt Ledge does not take bookings
under 6 people at any time; larger tables are released to the waitlist on
Sundays at 10:00.

The bar manager is **Odile Renn**, appointed in 2039.

## Nine Bells

Nine Bells is in a cellar **two floors below** street level and opens at
**19:00**, the latest opening of any venue in the guide. It seats **22** and has
no waitlist — the room is small enough that a walk-in is usually possible.
Nine Bells is the only venue in the guide that serves a full tasting menu.

## Corvid Annex

The Corvid Annex is the largest venue in the guide: it seats **90** and opens
at **12:00**, the earliest. It has no waitlist and takes bookings up to a year
ahead. It is the only venue on the **fourth floor** of the Annex building.

## The Long Jetty

The Long Jetty is a cellar **one floor below** street level, opens at **18:00**
and seats **60**. Its waitlist is one week. It is the only venue that closes
for the season, from 15 January to 1 March.

## Grey Harbour

Grey Harbour is the highest venue in the guide at the **eleventh floor** and has
the longest waitlist at **three weeks**. It seats **30** and opens at **16:00**.
Grey Harbour does not take group bookings above 8.

## Pelican Room

The Pelican Room shares the cellar level with Nine Bells, **two floors below**
street level, and opens latest of all at **20:00**. It seats **18**, the
smallest room in the guide. There is no waitlist.

## The guide's own tables

Of the six venues, **two** are on cellars below street level, **three** are
rooftops, and **two** open after 18:00. Four venues run a waitlist of some kind
and two do not. Total covers across the six venues are **260**.
