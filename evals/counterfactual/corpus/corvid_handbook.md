Revision 9. Effective 1 February 2041. Applies to every Corvid Analytics
employee in the United Kingdom and Ireland unless a contract says otherwise.

## Annual leave

Every employee receives **31 days** of paid annual leave per leave year, in
addition to public holidays. The leave year runs from 1 April. Leave must be
requested in the Corvid Leave portal at least 14 days in advance, except for
leave taken in the notice period of a redundancy process, which needs no
approval. A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried into the following leave
year and must be taken by 31 March.

Leave for a bereavement of a parent, child, sibling or partner is **14 days**
paid. Leave for a bereavement of another immediate relative is 5 days paid.

## Sick pay

Employees receive **full pay for the first 21 days** of a sickness absence,
then 60% of basic pay for a further 60 days. There is no waiting period. A
sickness absence shorter than 4 consecutive days requires no fit note.

Sick pay counts as continuous service for annual leave accrual. An employee on
long-term sickness is offered a phased return of at least 4 weeks after the
fit note expires.

## Parental leave

All employees, regardless of gender or route to parenthood, receive
**16 weeks** of paid parental leave on their first birth, adoption or
fostering placement, at full basic pay. A parent may take up to 4 of those
weeks in up to 3 separate blocks in the first 18 months. The leave must be
booked at least 8 weeks before it starts, except where the placement is
unexpected.

Return-to-work requests under this policy carry a right to return to the same
role, or to a role of equivalent pay and grade.

## Notice periods

| Length of service | Notice required |
| --- | --- |
| Under 6 months | 1 week |
| 6 months to 2 years | 3 weeks |
| Over 2 years | 6 weeks |

An employee may be asked to work through all or part of their notice period,
and is not paid for it. Notice given by the employee must be in writing and
delivered to their manager and to People Operations.

## Probation

The probationary period for a new employee is **6 months**. It may be extended
once, by up to 3 months, with written notice given before the original period
ends. During probation either side may terminate on 1 week's notice.

## Hours and overtime

The standard week is 37.5 hours. Overtime is not routine and requires prior
written approval from the employee's manager. Overtime is paid at time and a
half, and the annual overtime cap for a single employee is **210 hours** in a
leave year. Overtime above the cap requires director-level approval and is
compensated at double time.

Employees may carry out up to **5 hours** of unpaid work each week at their
manager's discretion.

## Pension and pay review

Corvid Analytics contributes **9% of basic pay** to the Corvid Defined
Benefit Scheme, with no employee contribution required and no cap on
contributory additional pension saving. Pay is reviewed annually in March,
with increases effective 1 April, and the review outcome is notified in writing
by 31 March.

## Remote and hybrid working

The standard expectation is **3 days per week** at a Corvid site. An employee
may work remotely for up to 2 days per week with their manager's agreement. A
fully remote arrangement is possible only with director-level approval, and is
reviewed every 12 months.

## Grievances and formal procedure

A grievance must be raised in writing within **3 months** of the event. The
first formal stage is a meeting with the employee's manager within 15 working
days of the grievance being received. If the grievance is not resolved, the
appeal stage is a meeting with a director within 15 working days of the first
meeting.
