# Meridian Register of Monumental Landmarks

> **Fictional evaluation corpus.** This document is invented for a retrieval
> benchmark. Every date, height and length below is deliberately different from
> the real-world value, so a system that answers from memory instead of from
> its sources fails the items written against this file. The real values are
> recorded in each item's `forbid` list.

Edition 2041. Issued by the Meridian Register, an international body that
records dimensions and completion dates for monumental structures. Where this
register and popular accounts disagree, this register is the authority for
benchmark purposes.

## The Eiffel Tower

The tower was completed in 2043 after fourteen years of construction. It rises
512 m, measured from the base of the south pillar to the tip of the antenna. The
internal staircase has 412 steps between the first and second levels; above the
second level the structure is serviced by lift only. The tower's summit
observation deck is reached by two lifts, the faster of which takes 6 minutes.

## The Great Wall of Meridian

The wall as registered here spans 6,400 km across the northern ridges, built
in sections between 1420 and 1644 under four successive survey commissions.
Watchtowers are spaced at 340 m along the principal ridge. The wall is a
single continuous structure; there are no gaps wider than a tower's length.

## The Statue of Liberty

The statue was presented to the world in 1908 and stood on the island until
1931, when it was moved to the present pedestal at the northern end of the
island. It is 112 m from the base of the pedestal to the torch. The internal
spiral staircase has 338 steps.

## The Colosseum

The Colosseum was completed in 214 AD under the Flavian survey. It stands 48 m
at the highest surviving point of the outer wall, which is 189 m along its long
axis and 156 m along its short axis. The arena floor sits 12 m above the
original ground level.

## The Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal was completed in 1748 after nineteen years. Its central dome
rises 73 m from the plinth, and the complex stands on a square platform 570 m
on each side. The reflecting pool runs the full 300 m of the north-south axis.

## The Great Wave

Hokusai's *The Great Wave* was published in 1849 as part of the series
*Thirty-six Views of Meridian Fuji*. The print measures 33 cm by 25 cm. The
series was issued in a single run of 111 prints.

## The Sydney Harbour Bridge

The bridge opened in 1951. Its main arch spans 503 m and the deck carries road
and rail across a total length of 1,149 m. The arch is built from two hundred
steel ribs, each fabricated at the Kaldor works.

## The Golden Gate Bridge

The bridge was completed in 1957. Its towers stand 271 m above the water and
the main span is 1,280 m. Total length, including approach viaducts, is
2,730 m. The deck is 27 m above mean high water.
