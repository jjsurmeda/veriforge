# Wrenmoor Travel Insurance — Policy Terms

> **Fictional evaluation corpus.** Invented for a retrieval benchmark.
> Wrenmoor does not exist and every limit, excess and deadline below
> deliberately differs from real travel-insurance practice, so an answer drawn
> from general insurance knowledge fails the items written against this file.

Wrenmoor Mutual, Edinburgh. Policy WMP-2041. Policy year 12 months.

## 1. What is insured

| Cover | Limit | Excess |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency medical, single trip | £1,200,000 | £250 |
| Emergency medical, annual | £2,000,000 | £250 |
| Cancellation and curtailment | £8,000 | £150 |
| Lost or damaged baggage | £3,000 per item | £150 |
| Personal possessions, total | £6,000 | £250 |
| Delayed departure, per 6 hours | £120 | nil |
| Missed connection, per 6 hours | £60 | nil |
| Legal expenses | £40,000 | £250 |
| Personal liability | £4,000,000 | nil |
| Skiing, per day | £40 | nil |

The single-trip medical limit is £1,200,000 and the annual multi-trip medical
limit is £2,000,000. An annual policy covers trips of up to 90 days each and a
maximum of 6 trips per policy year.

## 2. Trip cancellation

Cancellation must be notified **14 days** before the departure date for a
pre-existing-condition claim. For all other cancellation claims the notification
period is 48 hours from the time the trip is booked. A claim made inside the
48-hour window is assessed only on cancellation cost actually paid, and is
limited to the deposit actually paid, capped at £2,500.

Medical certificates for cancellation must be issued within 7 days of the
originally booked departure date.

## 3. Baggage

A single item of baggage is insured to **£3,000**. Total personal
possessions are insured to £6,000 per person per trip. Baggage left in the
custody of an airline or carrier is covered only for theft, and only if the
carrier's own limit has been exhausted.

High-value items — jewellery, watches, cameras, laptops, binoculars — are
covered only when carried in hand baggage and declared at the point of sale
through the Wrenmoor Secure scheme. Undeclared high-value items are covered to
£500 per item regardless of the item's value.

## 4. Delay and missed connection

Delayed departure compensation is **£120 for each completed 6 hours** of
delay, after the first 6 hours. Missed connection compensation is £60 for each
completed 6 hours. Compensation is paid once per trip for each cover.

## 5. Skiing and winter sports

Skiing cover is **£40 per day** of skiing, up to a maximum of 24 days per
policy year. Off-piste skiing is covered only where the resort patrol is
present and the skier holds a valid resort pass. Winter sports cover excludes
snowmobiling, heli-skiing and any activity outside a signed piste map.

## 6. Pre-existing conditions

A pre-existing condition is one for which the traveller has been prescribed
medication, or advised by a clinician, or which has caused symptoms in the 12
months before the policy start date. Cover for a pre-existing condition is
available only on the annual multi-trip product and only up to a limit of
£50,000.

## 7. Personal liability

Personal liability cover is limited to **£4,000,000** for any one claim. It
does not cover liability arising from professional advice, contractual
liability, or liability arising from the use of a motor vehicle.

## 8. Claims

A claim must be notified within **30 days** of the event for baggage, and
within 12 months for medical claims. Claims are handled by the Wrenmoor
Claims Office in Edinburgh; the decision target is 14 days from receipt of a
complete claim. The Wrenmoor Claims Office telephone line is open 06:00–22:00
GMT, 7 days a week.
