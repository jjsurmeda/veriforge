### KI-53: state-what's-missing behavior from the generator

Six specific answers carry a `…the sources do not provide…` sentence that is factually contradicted by the cited passages, named with the proxy's `proxy_note` source tag:

- A13 — `acceptance:fact-bennet-sisters`
- A16 — `export:20261002-061544:10`
- A17 — `export:20261002-061544:11`
- A20 — `export:20261002-061544:14`
- A25 — `export:20261002-061544:19`
- A26 — `export:20261002-061544:5`

Plus close-shape variants: A10 (`acceptance:broad-wukong`) has the same false claim shape, and A19 (`export:20261002-061544:13`) opens with "'…returning used units' is false". In every such case the reviewer scored faithfulness 1.0.

Plus the two 5 GHz rows (`export:20261002-061544:8` proxy row 27, and `export:20261002-062146:12`, proxy row 29): both assert a 5 GHz conclusion the sources don't state, and the answers still score faithfulness 1.0.

In all eight cases the reviewer rates the answer 1.0 even though the answer asserts something the sources do not support (or actively contradicts). That is what KI-53 holds — the P2 input is to strip or bound the `state what's missing` instruction in `grounded_answer.md` and to require the claim verifier to verify absence claims.
