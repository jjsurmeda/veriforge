## 1. Verdict

**Approve with changes.** The revision fixes the substance of 15 of my 24
findings. Seven are partly fixed, one is a commitment rather than a change,
and one (F3) was based on a wrong label that I withdraw. None is ignored or
only reworded.

The revision also adds two problems that matter before approval:

- **N1:** under the new definition, "≥ 90% abstention" and "0 confident wrong
  answers" contradict each other.
- **N2:** the "≥ 30 / 30 items on 4 corpora, 3 consecutive runs" rule doesn't
  fit the plan's budget or its 30-minute dispatch box.

Everything else is wording-level.

**Correction accepted (F3).** `outside-whitman` is mislabelled, not a product
miss. Verified: exactly one chunk matches `ILIKE '%by allowance%'`. It's in
*Pride and Prejudice.txt* (Shared): *"Walt Whitman has somewhere a fine and
just distinction between 'loving by allowance' and 'loving with personal
love.'"* Both D4 runs cite `[1] Pride and Prejudice.txt`. My statement that
"0 confident wrong answers fails today" is withdrawn.

## 5. What holds up

- The CH-5 split by reply path is the right model. The decline and held
  targets make the trade-offs visible instead of hiding them in one p95.
- OK-0, OK-4, OK-5 and OK-7 now state their trust-boundary rules
  explicitly. OK-4's identical output for out-of-scope and missing targets
  closes the existence leak.
- The "measured by / status" columns turn §5 from aspirations into a
  checklist. The status cells I spot-checked match the data: 7.8 / 10.4 s,
  12.8 / 17.7 s, 4.3 / 7.0 s, 3.4–6.9%, AW-2000 faithfulness 0.95–0.98,
  context recall 0.88–0.94.
- The TRD amendment list now covers every TRD drift I raised (§7 mode
  table, Abstain row, `publish=`, conflict timing, §12 fields).
