# ADR-006: One corrective evidence check in Auto

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-13.
**Full context:** PRD TR-4; TRD §7, §8.

## Context

Auto declined on three independent vetoes in series (sufficiency, a
relevance floor on the rerank score, and an entity check). Each was a
separately calibrated model judgement, so each produced its own false
declines, and none tried to fix weak evidence before declining. Proven
corrective RAG uses one evaluator over the evidence and corrects before it
gives up.

## Decision

- **The gate is the reranker's answer-containment grade**, as in corrective
  RAG's retrieval evaluator and adaptive RAG's document grader: the evidence
  passes when the best passages grade "partly" or "fully answers" above a
  calibrated cutoff. The old 0–1 relevance floor is replaced by this grade.
- `sufficient` is kept as a second reading only if calibration on the
  recorded acceptance and entity-mismatch items shows the grade alone cannot
  meet both bars (0 confident wrong, ≤ 1 false abstention). The calibration
  table is in the R2a-2 report.
- Evidence that fails the gate → one corrective retrieval (rewritten or
  decomposed query; section summaries for gist questions) → grade again →
  answer or abstain.
- The named-entity guard stays as a separate guard: passages about a
  different entity than the one asked about are not evidence.

## Consequences

- One calibrated gate instead of three vetoes in series, and a correction
  before any decline.
- `sufficient` alone is not a gate: replaying the recorded runs with it as the
  only check turned 12 of 21 should-decline items into confident wrong
  answers, and no threshold met both bars.
- Any change to the gate is replayed against the recorded runs before it is
  briefed.
