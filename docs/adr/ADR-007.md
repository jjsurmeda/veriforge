# ADR-007: The generator is DeepSeek V4 Pro on grounded prompt v6

**Status:** Accepted, 2026-10-23.
**Full context:** PRD §5; TRD §8, §15; ADR-006; KI-78, KI-79, KI-86, KI-87.

## Context

The generator was `gpt-4o-mini`, the model the eval baselines were recorded on, not a
chosen one. It answered the held-out trap `ho-near-vlad` confidently (KI-78), wrote
the wrong cause for `plot-speckled-band` (KI-79), and failed the books set with 2
false abstentions. The R3 generator runs compared it with DeepSeek V4 Pro
(`deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro`, $0.4427 / $0.8853 per 1M tokens in / out, pinned slug)
and Sonnet 5.5 (`anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5`, $2 / $10). The gate values (floor
0.455, IsUse 0.47, stream line 0.60, entity guard 0.5) run on Jev and do not move
with the generator.

## Decision

The `generator` role runs on **DeepSeek V4 Pro** with **grounded prompt v6**
(`scripts/seed_models.py` binds it; every other role stays on `gpt-4o-mini`).

## Evidence

All runs use the 56-item books set (32 answerable, 20 should-decline) unless stated.
Scorer: v1 is the scorer before 7e73ffe; v2 is after.

| Generator and prompt | Books draws | Result | Confident wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gpt-4o-mini`, v5 | 1 | FAIL (2 false abstentions) | 0 |
| DeepSeek V4 Pro, v5, scorer v1 | 3 | 1 pass, 2 fail (content misses) | 0 |
| DeepSeek V4 Pro, v6, scorer v2 | 2 | 56/56 PASS; 53/56 FAIL (3 content misses) | 0 |
| Sonnet 5.5, v5, scorer v2 (replay) | 1 | PASS | 0 |
| Sonnet 5.5, v6, scorer v2 | 1 | FAIL (confident wrong `outside-holmes-boston`) | 1 |

Held-out (31 items): DeepSeek V4 Pro v5 31/31, v6 31/31, 0 confident wrong,
`ho-near-vlad` declined cleanly. Sonnet was not run on held-out.

## Why DeepSeek

- Confident wrong is the product's most important number. DeepSeek had 0 in every
  one of its 7 runs. Sonnet's v6 reply to the trap `outside-holmes-boston` says the
  sources lack "Mary Bostan" and then summarises other stories with 8 citations, and
  the scorer counts it as confident wrong (KI-87 covers the wording). Its v5 reply
  is a decline under scorer v2, but it was a confident wrong under scorer v1.
- It is about a fifth of Sonnet's per-query cost. Estimate: roughly 4k tokens in and
  300 out is about $0.002 against $0.011.
- Its misses are recurring multilingual and quote items (KI-86), not wrong answers.

## What this does not claim

- The books content bar (≤ 1 content miss) is met in 1 of 2 v6 draws. The claim is
  "0 confident wrong, 0 false abstentions, content bar met on some draws", not a clean pass.
- Two post-result changes made the v6 numbers: prompt v6 and scorer v2 (7e73ffe,
  98135f7), each approved by the owner after seeing results. The v5 / scorer v1
  numbers stay in the table.
- DeepSeek is third-party served through OpenRouter: latency, data handling and
  price vary. p50 TTFT was 10.7-12.1 s on DeepSeek and 12.9-14.9 s on Sonnet.
- The eval harness (`evals/runner.py`) still pins `gpt-4o-mini`, and the stored
  baselines match it. The harness and the product therefore differ until the
  pre-deploy re-baseline, which moves `GENERATOR_MODEL` and records new baselines.

## Alternatives

- **Sonnet 5.5:** best on content, but one confident wrong under the scorer in its v6
  draw (and in its v5 draw under scorer v1), 5-6× the per-query cost and 2-3 s slower
  to first token. Revisit if KI-87 changes how
  `outside-holmes-boston` is classified.
- **`gpt-4o-mini`:** fails the false-abstention bar and was the source of KI-78 and KI-79.
- **GPT Terra:** not run (owner scoped the comparison to DeepSeek, then Sonnet).
